# Pricing

Decided on 24 November 2026.

## The model

Kleidi is priced **per sale, paid by the firm**: about **€59 for each sale opened**, with unlimited lawyers and staff. The price includes:

- the buyer portal, with next steps, the payment schedule and messages with the lawyer;
- English explanations of shared documents from Kleidi Assist, up to 25 per sale;
- English, Greek and Russian throughout;
- EU hosting, virus scanning and the audit trail.

Firms usually charge buyers per matter, so they can pass this on as a disbursement or absorb it.

| Plan         | Who it suits                                   | Price                 |
| ------------ | ---------------------------------------------- | --------------------- |
| Pilot        | A firm trying Kleidi on two to five live sales | Free, with onboarding |
| Firm         | Most firms                                     | €59 per sale opened   |
| Multi-office | Groups with several offices or countries       | Quoted                |

## Why this model

- **It matches how firms charge.** Cypriot property lawyers usually charge a buyer a fixed fee of about €700 to €2,500, or about 1 to 1.5% of the price. €59 is a small share of that fee.
- **It fits small firms.** Per-seat tools cost about $49 to $150 per lawyer per month. That model would make a small firm with a few lawyers and many sales pay little, and a large firm with few sales pay a lot. Per-sale pricing follows the value delivered instead.
- **It keeps the buyer as the lawyer's client.** We considered charging the buyer directly, but rejected it because the buyer is the firm's client.
- **The running cost is small.** Hosting starts at €15 to €18 a month on one server. Each English explanation costs cents. The cap of 25 explanations per sale keeps the AI cost of one case bounded.

## Market figures

These figures come from secondary sources, checked on 24 November 2026:

- About 18,000 property sales were recorded in Cyprus in 2025, and about 7,300 of them were to foreign buyers.
- Buyers' legal fees are about 1 to 1.5% of the price plus VAT, or a fixed €700 to €2,500 for standard resales.
- Practice-management software costs about $49 to $150 per user per month.
